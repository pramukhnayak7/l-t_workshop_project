CAMPUS COMPASS SAMPLE COLLEGE HANDBOOK

Attendance
Students are expected to attend at least 75 percent of scheduled sessions in every course. Students who fall below the attendance requirement should contact their course coordinator promptly. Approved accommodations and documented exceptional circumstances are reviewed by the academic office.

Examinations
Students should arrive at the examination venue at least 15 minutes before the published start time and bring their college identification card. Mobile phones and unauthorized notes must be stored away during the examination. Students who need examination accommodations should contact the registrar before the examination period and provide supporting documentation. The registrar confirms arrangements directly with the student.

Assessment and coursework
Course-specific assessment weights, coursework deadlines, and resubmission rules are published in the relevant syllabus. Students should confirm dates with their course instructor and the current learning platform. Extensions must be requested before a published deadline and are subject to instructor approval.

Internships
Students planning an internship should contact the careers office and their academic adviser before accepting a placement. Internship eligibility, credit, insurance, and reporting requirements depend on the student's programme. Students must receive written approval before counting an internship toward academic credit.

Student support
The student services office can direct students to academic advising, counselling, accessibility support, financial aid, and wellbeing services. Students with urgent safety concerns should contact campus security or local emergency services.

Course information
Official learning outcomes, required texts, weekly topics, and course assessment details appear in each approved course syllabus. When a syllabus and this general handbook differ on a course-specific detail, ask the course instructor or department office for the current rule.
